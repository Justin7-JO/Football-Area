Mohamed Salah

Mohamed Salah is one of the greatest football players in the history of Egypt and Africa. He was born on June 15, 1992, in the village of Nagrig, which is located in Basyoun Center in Gharbia Governorate. From a young age, he loved football and used to travel for long hours every day from his village to Cairo to train and pursue his dream of becoming a professional player. He began his football career with El Mokawloon El Arab Club, where he showed great talent that attracted the attention of European clubs.


in 2012, Mohamed Salah moved to FC Basel in Switzerland, where he achieved great success and helped his team win the Swiss League. He also impressed everyone with his performances in European competitions. After that, he joined Chelsea in England, then played for Fiorentina and Roma in Italy. During his time at Roma, he had some of his best seasons and became one of the top scorers in the Italian league.



In 2017, Mohamed Salah joined Liverpool FC, which was the most important step in his career. He performed brilliantly and scored a record number of goals in his first season in the English Premier League. He played a key role in helping Liverpool win the UEFA Champions League in 2019 and the English Premier League in 2020 after a 30-year absence.

Mohamed Salah has won many individual awards, including the African Player of the Year award several times and the Premier League Golden Boot more than once. He also led the Egyptian national team to the final of the Africa Cup of Nations and helped Egypt qualify for the 2018 World Cup after a long absence. Mohamed Salah is known for his good manners, humility, and charitable work. He is a role model for young people and a symbol of success through hard work and determination, and he has raised Egypt’s name high on the international stage.
